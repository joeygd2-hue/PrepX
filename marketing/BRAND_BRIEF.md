# PrepX brand brief (for anyone making PrepX ads)

Paste this whole file into your AI assistant before asking it for ads, scripts, captions or designs. It describes what PrepX is, how it looks, how it talks, and the rules every ad has to follow.

---

## 1. What PrepX is

**PrepX is a free competition-prep app for high school students in DECA, FBLA, HOSA and TSA.**

- **One-liner:** One place to prepare for DECA, FBLA, HOSA and TSA.
- **Audience:** High school students (about 14–18) who compete in career and technical student organizations (CTSOs), plus their chapter officers and advisers.
- **The problem:** Prep is scattered. Students hunt through old PDFs, random quiz sites and group chats, and rarely get to practice a roleplay or presentation out loud before the real thing.
- **The promise:** Learn it, practice it and rehearse it out loud, all in one app, for whichever event you compete in.
- **Price:** **Free.** PrepX launches free. Don't mention paid plans, tiers, "Pro" or prices anywhere.
- **Website/domain:** prep-x.app is bought but not live yet. **Don't show a URL, handle or QR code** in ads until it is. End on the PrepX logo plus "Free to start" (or just "Free").
- **Platform:** A web app that installs to the phone home screen like an app (a PWA). Don't say "download on the App Store" or "Google Play".

## 2. What's actually in the app (only advertise these)

| Feature | What the student sees |
|---|---|
| **AI judge roleplays** | Pick your event, get a realistic scenario, answer **out loud** by voice, then get a practice score out of 100 with feedback. There's also a face-to-face mode and a drill of likely judge follow-up questions. |
| **Full practice exams** | Timed, test-style exams per event (for example, 100 questions), plus a "test-day rehearsal" mode with no pausing that auto-submits. |
| **Adaptive Quick 10** | 10 questions at your level, weighted toward your weak topics, with instant explanations. |
| **Lessons (a full course)** | A flip-through reader with paged lessons, highlighted key terms with pop-up definitions, short checks, and progress rings per unit. |
| **Mistake notebook** | Every missed question stays here until you've got it right. |
| **Flashcards & 60-second sprint** | Key terms; a sprint is as many questions as you can answer in a minute. |
| **Explain it back** | Say a key term in your own words and AI checks it. |
| **Ask AI** | Highlight any text for an explanation, or type a question. |
| **AI coach** | Chat about concepts, indicators and strategy. |
| **Bookmarks** | Save questions, add notes, practice your saved set. |
| **Head-to-head duels** | Challenge a friend with a code; you both get the same questions and compare scores. |
| **Study planner & focus mode** | Pick study days and minutes per day, set a reminder time; focus mode is a 25-minute countdown ring. |
| **Streaks, XP, levels, readiness score** | Daily streak, XP for correct answers, levels, and a readiness score for your event. |
| **Chapter features** | Join your chapter with a code; officers open a meeting check-in and members tap to check in (earning XP). There's also a chapter leaderboard, quiz night and a calendar. |
| **Competition calendar & countdown** | Your competition date and a day-by-day plan leading up to it. |

**Content scale (safe to quote):** 4 organizations, **380+ lessons, 3,000+ practice questions, 1,900+ flashcards**, covering events across all four orgs.

## 3. Honesty rules (non-negotiable)

1. **Not affiliated.** PrepX is independent. Never imply it's official, endorsed by, or made by DECA, FBLA, HOSA or TSA. Never use their logos; only their names, in plain text.
2. **Practice scores aren't real scores.** AI judge scores are *practice* scores. Never say "guaranteed to win", "official score" or "real judges".
3. **Original content.** All questions and lessons are written by PrepX. Never say or show that PrepX has "real past tests" or "actual exam questions".
4. **No fake proof.** No invented testimonials, user counts, ratings, "X students won States", or winner claims. Use numbers only from section 2.
5. **No competitor callouts.** Don't name or show other prep apps, and don't copy another brand's exact visuals or copy.
6. **No prices**, as noted in section 1.
7. **Teens are the audience.** Keep it encouraging and school-appropriate. No fear-mongering, no shaming people who lose, nothing edgy for its own sake.

## 4. Voice

- **Confident, calm, a little competitive.** It sounds like a sharp upperclassman who's already been to Nationals, not a corporate brand or a hype-bro.
- **Short.** Headlines are 2–5 words, usually ending in a period: "Practice roleplays out loud." "Climb the leaderboard." "Walk in ready."
- **Second person, present tense.** "You", not "users" or "students".
- **Emphasize one or two words per headline** in the serif italic (see section 5): "Full practice *exams.*"
- Use **plain words**, not jargon. Say "practice exam", not "assessment module".
- Org names are always caps: **DECA, FBLA, HOSA, TSA**.

Lines that fit the voice:
- "Four competitions. One place to *prepare.*"
- "Practice roleplays *out loud.*"
- "Scores *up.* Streak *up.* Rank *up.*"
- "Walk in *ready.*"
- "Free to start."

## 5. Visual identity: "Graphite & Volt"

The look is quiet, dark and premium, with a single electric lime accent for the moments that matter.

### Colors (ads use the dark theme)

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#09090A` | Near-black canvas |
| Card | `#111113` (gradient to `#141416`) | Raised panels, app screen mock-ups |
| Panel / chip | `#151517`, `#1F1F22` | Inner panels, tracks |
| Hairline | `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` / `0.16` | Card borders |
| Text | `#F2F2EC` | Warm off-white |
| Muted text | `#8D8D86` | Labels, captions |
| **Volt (brand accent)** | **`#C6F03A`** | One accent per scene: highlights, the logo arrow, glows, "you are here" |
| Success | `#4FD18B` | Correct marks |

**Org colors**: use these to tint a scene that's about that org (glows, accents, the serif headline word):

| Org | Accent | Glow |
|---|---|---|
| DECA | `#5B9BFF` | `#2F6FD6` |
| FBLA | `#F6C34A` | `#C99A1C` |
| HOSA | `#F0668F` | `#C23B6A` |
| TSA  | `#FF5C70` | `#E0303F` |

Rules: one accent color per scene. Lime is the PrepX brand color; org colors are for org-specific moments. Never put lime text on a light background.

### Type

- **Geist** (sans), weight 600, tight tracking (about −0.045em), for headlines and UI.
- **Geist Mono**, uppercase with wide tracking (about 0.16em) and small size, for labels and "eyebrows" (e.g. `AI JUDGE · DECA · HOSPITALITY`), and for numbers.
- **Instrument Serif *Italic*** for one or two emphasis words per headline, colored with the scene accent.

All three fonts are free on Google Fonts.

### UI mock-ups in ads

Show the app as clean, simplified cards in the dark theme, using the same card language as the app:
- Rounded cards (radius about 24–28px), hairline borders, soft deep shadow.
- A mono eyebrow label at the top, a short title, then **one** interaction (a score counting up, a correct answer turning green, a key term highlighting, a rank moving up).
- Use realistic but invented example data (names like "Maya R.", "Daniel K.") and original example questions.

### Atmosphere

- A soft, blurred color glow behind the content (lime, or the scene's org color).
- Very light film grain and a dark vignette at the edges.
- Lots of empty space. One idea on screen at a time.

## 6. Motion language

- **Blur-in, blur-out.** Elements enter rising about 30px from 14px blur to sharp; they exit upward with blur. Use ease-out on entry and ease-in-out on exit.
- **Word-by-word headline reveals**, staggered about 70ms apart.
- **Everything lands on the beat.** Scene changes happen on bar lines; small interactions (a tap, a highlight, a rank jump) happen on individual beats.
- **A subtle camera pulse** of about 1% scale on each beat, and a bigger kick plus a lime flash on the drop.
- **The "up" motif.** Things rise: scores count up, ranks move up, lines stack upward. It fits the logo's arrow.
- **Logo reveal:** the square scales in with a slight overshoot, the X strokes draw on, the volt arrow draws last, then "Prep*X*" slides out beside it.

## 7. The proven ad formula (from PrepX ad #1)

Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, about 16–18 seconds, no voiceover. The text on screen carries the message.

| Beat | Scene |
|---|---|
| Hook | DECA / FBLA / HOSA / TSA pop in, each over its org-color glow. "Four competitions." |
| Promise | "One place to *prepare.*" |
| Feature 1 | AI judge card: a voice waveform, then a score counting up to the 90s |
| Feature 2 | Practice exam card: tap the right answer, it turns correct, "+10 XP" floats up |
| Feature 3 | Lesson reader: key terms highlight, the page flips, a definition pops up |
| Feature 4 | Leaderboard: "You" climbs to #1 one beat at a time |
| Build | "Scores *up.* Streak *up.* Rank *up.*" stacking on the last beats |
| Drop | Lime flash, then the PrepX logo reveal, "Free to start.", and the four org dots |

**Music:** ad #1 is cut to a 123 BPM track ("up up up"): a beat every ~0.49s, a new scene every 4-beat bar (~1.95s), and the logo on the drop at ~13.3s. If you use a trending TikTok sound, export the video silent and add the sound inside TikTok.

**TikTok safe zone (1080×1920):** keep text and key UI out of the **right ~140px** (the like/comment/share buttons), the **bottom ~420px** (caption and username) and the **top ~130px**. Ad #1 scales its content to 93% and shifts it up and left to stay clear.

## 8. Ad checklist

- [ ] Says or implies **free**; no prices or plans.
- [ ] No URL or handle (the site isn't live yet).
- [ ] No org logos and no "official"; scores are "practice" scores.
